Elevator Pitch: Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

In short: Elevator Pitch has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We don't collect, store or sell any of your data. Your projects stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, and the app's AI features run on your device. The only time anything leaves your device through the app is when you choose to upload a video to your YouTube channel.

This policy covers the Elevator Pitch app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, made by Atomic 42 Inc. ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, no analytics or tracking tools, no advertising, and no servers of ours that it talks to. We never see your projects, recordings, photos or scripts.

Your projects

  • Projects (scenes, text, drawings, pictures, voiceovers and music) are saved on your device.
  • On Apple devices, if you're signed in to iCloud, projects (and drawings and pictures you save to My Clip Art) sync between your devices through your own iCloud Drive. Apple handles that storage under Apple's privacy policy; we can't access it.
  • Projects and videos are shared only when you export or share them yourself, to wherever you choose.

Microphone, photos and files

  • Microphone: used only while you record a voiceover. The recording is saved in your project.
  • Photos: pictures you pick are copied into your project. If you save a finished video to your photo library, the app adds just that video.
  • Files: the app reads only the files you choose to import, and writes only where you choose to export.

You can turn any of these permissions off at any time in your device's settings.

AI features

Drafting scenes from a description, narrating your script, and cutting the background out of a photo all run on your device: Apple Intelligence on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and the on-device models from Google's ML Kit on Android. Your text and pictures aren't sent to us. On Android, ML Kit is part of Google Play services, which is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Uploading to YouTube

Uploading is optional and happens only when you ask for it.

  • You sign in with Google in Google's own sign-in screen. The app asks only for permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It can't read your channel, other videos, email or contacts.
  • The app keeps the sign-in token securely on your device (in the Keychain on Apple devices) so you don't need to sign in each time. You can sign out of YouTube in the app, and remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The video, title, description and privacy setting you choose are sent directly from your device to YouTube. They don't pass through any server of ours. YouTube's handling of them is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's privacy policy.
  • Elevator Pitch's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Feedback and ratings

Send Feedback in Help opens a new email in your own mail app, with the app version and your device model and system version filled in. You can change or remove anything before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use what you email us only to reply and to improve the app. The app may also show Apple's standard rating prompt; Apple handles any rating or review you leave.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
