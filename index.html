Get help

In the app, open Help (?) and choose Send Feedback… (on the Mac, it's also in the Help menu), or email pawan.singh.5@gmail.com, and we'll get back to you. It helps to say which device you're using and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps too.

If you're testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app, or take a screenshot in Elevator Pitch and TestFlight will offer to send it.

Common questions

  • How do I start? Tap ? for How-To Videos and choose Edit a Copy on an example, or start from a template.
  • Where are my projects? On your device, and in iCloud Drive when you're signed in to iCloud. They sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • YouTube upload says my account has no channel. Open YouTube with that Google account, create a channel, then upload again.
  • How do I stop YouTube access? Use Sign Out of YouTube in the app, or remove Elevator Pitch at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
